### Lời giải

<MdxFormula formula={"\\begin{aligned}P(\\text{Queen}\\mid\\text{Face}) &= \\frac{P(\\text{Face}\\mid\\text{Queen})P(\\text{Queen})}{P(\\text{Face})} \\\\ &= \\frac{1\\times\\frac{1}{13}}{\\frac{3}{13}} \\\\ &= \\frac{1}{3}\\end{aligned}"} />

## Bài tập 2: Không dùng Laplace

Cho dữ liệu sau.

### Lời giải

**Bước 1: Tính các xác suất thành phần**

<MdxFormula formula={"P(\\text{Yes}) = \\frac{9}{14}"} />

<MdxFormula formula={"P(\\text{No}) = \\frac{5}{14}"} />

**Bước 2: Tính các xác suất có điều kiện**

### Không dùng Laplace

Lưu ý: rất dễ nhầm <MdxFormula inline formula={"P(\\text{Sunny}\\mid\\text{Yes})"} /> là <MdxFormula inline formula={"\\frac{2}{14}"} />.

**Outlook**

<MdxFormula formula={"\\begin{array}{c|cc} & \\text{Yes} & \\text{No} \\\\ \\hline \\text{Sunny} & \\frac{2}{9} & \\frac{3}{5} \\\\ \\text{Overcast} & \\frac{4}{9} & \\frac{0}{5} \\\\ \\text{Rain} & \\frac{3}{9} & \\frac{2}{5}\\end{array}"} />

**Temp**

<MdxFormula formula={"\\begin{array}{c|cc} & \\text{Yes} & \\text{No} \\\\ \\hline \\text{Hot} & \\frac{2}{9} & \\frac{2}{5} \\\\ \\text{Mild} & \\frac{4}{9} & \\frac{2}{5} \\\\ \\text{Cool} & \\frac{3}{9} & \\frac{1}{5}\\end{array}"} />

**Humidity**

<MdxFormula formula={"\\begin{array}{c|cc} & \\text{Yes} & \\text{No} \\\\ \\hline \\text{High} & \\frac{3}{9} & \\frac{4}{5} \\\\ \\text{Normal} & \\frac{6}{9} & \\frac{1}{5}\\end{array}"} />

**Wind**

<MdxFormula formula={"\\begin{array}{c|cc} & \\text{Yes} & \\text{No} \\\\ \\hline \\text{Weak} & \\frac{6}{9} & \\frac{2}{5} \\\\ \\text{Strong} & \\frac{3}{9} & \\frac{3}{5}\\end{array}"} />

<MdxFormula formula={"\\operatorname{score}(\\text{Yes}\\mid\\text{Sunny, Strong}) = P(\\text{Strong}\\mid\\text{Yes})P(\\text{Sunny}\\mid\\text{Yes})P(\\text{Yes}) = \\frac{3}{9}\\times\\frac{2}{9}\\times\\frac{9}{14} = \\frac{1}{21}"} />

<MdxFormula formula={"\\operatorname{score}(\\text{No}\\mid\\text{Sunny, Strong}) = P(\\text{Strong}\\mid\\text{No})P(\\text{Sunny}\\mid\\text{No})P(\\text{No}) = \\frac{3}{5}\\times\\frac{3}{5}\\times\\frac{5}{14} = \\frac{9}{70}"} />

Vậy ta có thể đưa ra dự đoán cho Sunny, Strong là No.

</MdxPage>

FAQ low-risk đi fast model nếu eval pass; refund recommendation đi high-quality route nhưng action vẫn cần approval. Same conversation giữ sticky route trừ failover compatible.

| Stage | FAQ low-risk | Refund recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Hard capability | Text + citation | Schema + evidence + approval handoff |
| Trust/privacy | Tenant policy pass | Tenant policy pass |
| Health/quota | Candidate breaker closed, reservation fits | Candidate breaker closed, reservation fits |
| Eval gate | Fast route đạt quality/safety threshold | High-quality route đạt stricter threshold |
| Route record | `policy-v7`, sticky conversation | `policy-v7`, sticky conversation |
| Side effect | Không có | Backend/human approval; model không tự refund |

Nếu không candidate nào qua mọi hard gate, router trả `no_safe_route`; nó không âm thầm bỏ schema, privacy hay approval để tăng availability.

<LessonNote>Cost/latency chỉ xếp hạng các route đã đạt capability, policy và eval gates. Fallback là route riêng cần conformance và canary evidence.</LessonNote>
